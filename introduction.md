# Introduction

## What is Lokalist?

::: info
**Lokalist** is a curated, community-driven directory dedicated to showcasing the digital landscape of Malaysia. From massive government systems to niche open-source tools, we collect and categorize projects that share one common DNA: **They are built by Malaysians.**
<br><br>
In a world dominated by global tech giants, Lokalist serves as a dedicated space to discover local alternatives, support homegrown innovation, and celebrate the technical talent within our borders.
:::

---

## Our Mission

Our goal is simple: **Visibility.** Many Malaysians are building world-class software, yet these projects often go unnoticed by the local public. Lokalist aims to bridge that gap by providing a central "Yellow Pages" for the modern Malaysian web.

### What we include:
* **Government Systems:** Public portals and digital services that power our nation.
* **Commercial Products:** SaaS, E-commerce platforms, and Fintech built locally.
* **Open Source Tools:** Libraries, scripts, and frameworks contributed by Malaysian devs.
* **Experimental Projects:** Innovative "Build in Public" projects from the community.

---

## Why "Lokal"?

We believe in the **#SupportLokal** movement. By choosing a Malaysian-made system or app, you are:
1.  **Supporting the Local Economy:** Keeping digital spend within the country.
2.  **Encouraging Innovation:** Giving local founders the traction they need to scale.
3.  **Building Community:** Connecting local developers with local users.

---

## Built by the Community, for the Community

Lokalist is a project maintained by **Soluna**. 

As a team dedicated to web development services, we at **Soluna** wanted to create a platform that doesn't just offer services, but highlights the incredible work already being done by our peers across the country.

::: tip PROUDLY MALAYSIAN
Every entry in this list is verified to ensure it is a local product or founded by Malaysians. If you see something missing, we encourage you to help us grow this directory.
:::

---

## How it Works

Lokalist is a **static directory**. This means everything you see here is hardcoded and version-controlled. 

* **Fast:** No heavy databases, just lightning-fast Markdown.
* **Transparent:** Anyone can see how the list is organized.
* **Searchable:** Use the search bar at the top (`Ctrl + K`) to find any local project instantly.
