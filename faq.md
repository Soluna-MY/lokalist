# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Lokalist and how we manage the Malaysian project directory.

---

## General Questions

### What is the main goal of Lokalist?
Lokalist exists to solve the "discovery problem" for Malaysian tech. We want to make it easy for anyone to find local software alternatives, whether it's a payment gateway for a business or a fun open-source project for a developer.

### Is Lokalist a government initiative?
No. Lokalist is a private community initiative maintained by **Soluna**. While we list government systems (like MySejahtera or public portals) to provide a complete view of our digital ecosystem, we are not affiliated with any government agency.

### Why is it a static site?
We chose a static approach (using VitePress) because it is incredibly fast, secure, and easy for the community to contribute to via GitHub. It also ensures the site remains lightweight for users on mobile data.

---

## Submissions & Criteria

### How do you define a "Malaysian Project"?
To be listed on Lokalist, a project must meet at least one of these criteria:
1. **Founded by Malaysians:** The creator or lead founder is a Malaysian citizen.
2. **Local Product:** The company is registered and headquartered in Malaysia.
3. **National Service:** Systems built specifically for the Malaysian public infrastructure.

### Does it cost anything to be listed?
No. Listing on Lokalist is **completely free**. We do not accept payment for rankings or "featured" spots. Our goal is to stay unbiased and useful.

### How can I add my project?
Currently, you can submit a project by:
* Creating a **Pull Request** on our GitHub repository.
* Reaching out to the **Soluna** team through our official channels.
*(A dedicated submission form is coming soon!)*

---

## Technical & Legal

### Can I use the data on Lokalist for my own project?
Yes! Lokalist is a community resource. Since the list is hardcoded in Markdown on our GitHub, you can reference the data, provided you attribute the source back to Lokalist.

### My project is listed here but I want it removed. What do I do?
We aim to respect the wishes of all local makers. If you are the owner of a project and wish for it to be removed or updated, please contact us at **Soluna** or open an issue on GitHub, and we will handle it immediately.

### Who is Soluna?
**Soluna** (under ZA Soluna Enterprise) is a web development brand focused on building high-quality digital systems. Lokalist is our "Community Give-back" project to help grow the Malaysian tech scene.
