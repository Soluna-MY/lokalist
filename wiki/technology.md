# Technology

Here is a list of technology projects, web apps, and mobile apps made by Malaysians, categorized by their respective industries.

## Fintech & Insurtech

*   **[PolicyStreet](https://www.policystreet.com/)**: An insurance marketplace that allows users to compare and purchase insurance products.
*   **[Touch 'n Go eWallet](https://www.touchngo.com.my/)**: A versatile e-wallet used for tolls, payments, money transfers, and more.
*   **[MAE by Maybank2u](https://www.maybank2u.com.my/maybank2u/malaysia/en/personal/services/digital_banking/mae_app.page)**: An all-in-one lifestyle and e-wallet app by Maybank.
*   **[Boost](https://www.myboost.com.my/)**: One of the leading e-wallets in Malaysia for cashless payments.

## Digital Health

*   **[MySejahtera](https://mysejahtera.malaysia.gov.my/)**: An application developed by the Malaysian Government for COVID-19 management, now evolving to support broader digital health transformation.
*   **[Doc2Us](https://doc2us.com/)**: A telemedicine app that connects patients with doctors online.

## Automotive & Data

*   **[Katsana](https://katsana.com/)**: A data company focusing on connected cars and telematics for fleet management and insurance.
*   **[Carsome](https://www.carsome.my/)**: An online platform for buying and selling used cars.

## Platforms & Marketplaces

*   **[Jobstreet.com](https://www.jobstreet.com.my/)**: One of the earliest and largest job portals in Malaysia.
*   **[Kaodim](https://www.kaodim.com/)**: A marketplace that connects users with professional services such as cleaning, repairs, and more.
*   **[Eatigo](https://eatigo.com/my/kuala-lumpur/en)**: A restaurant reservation app that offers time-based discounts.
*   **[HappyFresh](https://www.happyfresh.my/)**: An online grocery delivery platform.
*   **[StoreHub](https://www.storehub.com/)**: Provides an omnichannel retail management system for physical and online stores.

## Super Apps

*   **[Grab](https://www.grab.com/my)**: A "super app" that offers transportation, food delivery, and payment services.
*   **[AirAsia MOVE](https://www.airasia.com/en/gb)**: Functions as a travel app, ride-hailing service, and shopping platform.
