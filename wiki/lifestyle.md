# Lifestyle
This page lists lifestyle-related projects by Malaysian creators.

## Health & Wellness
* [**Naluri**](https://www.naluri.life/) — A digital health service provider that offers professional mental and physical health coaching. <Badge type="info" text="Digital Health" />
* [**The Mind Faculty**](https://www.themindfaculty.com/) — A private mental health clinic providing a wide range of psychiatric, psychological, and counseling services. <Badge type="info" text="Mental Health" />

## Arts & Culture
* [**Pusaka**](https://www.pusaka.org/) — A non-profit organisation that works to support the viability of traditional Malaysian arts. <Badge type="info" text="Heritage" />
* [**The REX KL**](https://www.rexkl.com/) — A multi-concept hub that hosts a variety of cultural, recreational, and F&B events. <Badge type="info" text="Event Space" />

## Travel & Tourism
* [**Loka**](https://www.loka.my/area/my) - A platform to discover Malaysia, offering neighborhood guides and cultural insights. <Badge type="info" text="Local Guide" />
* [**Catch Up!**](https://catchupmobility.com/) - A smart Malaysia train navigation companion. <Badge type="info" text="Public Transport" />
* [**Cari Pasar Malam**](https://pasarmalam.app/) - Explore Malaysian night markets. <Badge type="info" text="Local Guide" />

## Food & Beverage
* [**Kerisik**](https://www.kerisik.app/) - A recipe manager app to organize recipes, plan meals, and generate grocery lists. <Badge type="info" text="Recipe Manager" />

## Wedding
* [**JomKahwin**](https://jomkahwin.blog/) - A platform to find wedding vendors in Malaysia. <Badge type="info" text="Wedding Vendor" />
