This page lists finance and fintech projects by Malaysian creators.

::: info PROFIT RATES
Profit rates in digital banks are often promotional.
:::

* [**AEON Bank**](https://aeonbank.com.my) — Fully Digital. (Shariah-Compliant). Profit: 0.25% p.a. base (up to 3.0% p.a. in Savings Pots). <Badge type="info" text="Islamic" />
* [**Boost Bank**](https://myboostbank.co) — Fully Digital. (Shariah-Compliant). Profit: 2.5% p.a. base (up to 4.0% p.a. in Special Jars). <Badge type="info" text="Islamic" />
* [**Ryt Bank**](https://rytbank.my/) — Fully Digital. (Shariah-Compliant). Profit: Up to 4.0% p.a. savings account. <Badge type="warning" text="AI-Enhanced" />
* [**KAF Digital Bank**](https://www.kaf.com.my) — Fully Digital. (Shariah-Compliant). Profit: Competitive Hibah returns (TBA). <Badge type="info" text="Islamic" />
